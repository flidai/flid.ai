03 / SCALE & CLEAR SPACE

One mark, every time.

Use the exact 12-layer primary mark at every size. Never substitute another layer count. Scale the complete artwork proportionally; never resize its paths or reapply its stroke. For small platform surfaces, use the supplied optically compensated raster exports rather than redrawing or simplifying the field.

CLEAR SPACE0.25D

Keep at least one quarter of the mark diameter (D) clear on every side. The mark-to-word gap is 0.20D.

05 / COLOR

Primer primitives, deliberately constrained.

Flid uses a small semantic subset of Primer’s dark and light themes. Canvas and foreground carry the identity; accent blue is for interaction and signal, not a required part of the primary logo.

06 / USE & MISUSE

Protect the signal.

Consistency comes from a short set of rules, applied every time.

DO
  • Use the lowercase flid wordmark.
  • Preserve the generated geometry and clear space.
  • Use monochrome ink or paper for the primary logo.
  • Use the supplied 12-layer raster exports for small platform surfaces.
DON'T
  • Add .ai or AI to the primary wordmark.
  • Place the open field inside an arbitrary circle.
  • Stretch, crop, rotate, or redraw individual marks.
  • Use accent decoratively across the entire logo.
07 / CANONICAL ASSET LIBRARY

Generated once. Used everywhere.

The approved 12-layer mark uses a tight artwork box. Its constructed curves and Geist SemiBold wordmark are expanded into filled vector outlines, so every SVG scales proportionally and renders identically without requiring the font to be installed.

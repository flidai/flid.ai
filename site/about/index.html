What stands behind the products and the consulting work.

  • A Danish company

    Flid AI ApS, CVR 43463217, in Odense. You work directly with the people who build the products.

  • Runs where you choose

    LeapView runs on your own infrastructure. Your data, models and access rules stay in your systems and your Git repository.

  • Read-only on your systems

    Alloy Catalog never changes your CAD files, vault or ERP. Native CAD files stay on your network.

  • Open, no lock-in

    LeapView is open source under Apache-2.0. Alloy Catalog exports everything as CSV or JSON, and your data is never used to train AI models.

Flid / About

A Danish product labbuilding durable systems.

Founded in Odense, Flid builds software where people and agents work through the same governed data, capabilities, and evidence.

The name

Diligence is the operating system.

Independent by design, Flid stays close to the products, the technical decisions, and the people using them.

Flid is Danish for diligence—the care, persistence, and attention behind work made to last.

We apply that standard to agent-native software: governed foundations, explicit actions, and evidence that people can inspect.

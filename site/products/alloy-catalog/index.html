The problem

Product knowledge is spread everywhere.

Parts, drawings and assemblies in the vault; item numbers and stock in the ERP; the rest in people’s heads.

  • Searching for the right file

    Engineers dig through vault folders and guess at file names. The newest revision is somewhere, if you know where to look.

  • Redesigning parts you already have

    When a part can’t be found, it gets drawn again, and each duplicate adds a part number, a drawing and stock to manage.

  • Answers that live in people’s heads

    Where is this part used? Which variant is current? Sales, service and purchasing wait for engineering, and the knowledge leaves when people do.

Features

One place for every part, and every answer.

  • Read-only sources

    Reads a Vault’s SQL database, set up for each deployment, or Inventor files (.ipt, .iam, .idw, .ipn) on a file share. It does not change them.

  • ERP matching

    Files are matched to the ERP item master and transactions through a pipeline set up for each company.

  • One page per part

    Key facts with their sources, previews, a 3D view where a STEP or DWF file exists, bill of materials, where-used, drawings, ERP items and stock, classification, people and history.

  • Search

    Search by what a part is, what it is made of or where it is used, not by file name. Facets, columns, and quick search with ⌘K.

  • Data quality

    An overview score, ERP consistency checks, and lists of conflicts between CAD and ERP, so gaps are shown rather than hidden.

  • Built-in AI agent

    Answers questions about parts and assemblies and cites every document it names. A read-only MCP endpoint lets other AI assistants read the catalog with each person’s own access.

How it works

An alloy of your engineering data.

  1. Connect read-only

    We connect to your vault and ERP. They stay exactly as they are; Alloy Catalog only reads them.

  2. Read everything

    Every model, drawing and bill of materials, including the details nobody had time to type in.

  3. Combine per part

    One page per part number, with every fact traced to its source and gaps shown plainly.

  4. Put it to work

    Teams search, ask and build on it, from the design office to the service desk.

Who it is for

Everyone who touches a part.

  • Engineering

    Find, compare and reuse parts instead of redrawing them, and see the impact of a change before making it.

  • Production and purchasing

    See the bill of materials, where a part is used and what is in stock, without opening CAD.

  • Sales and service

    Answer questions about parts and spares directly, instead of waiting for engineering.

  • Management

    Clean, connected product data: the base for spare-parts portals, configurators and AI.

Your data

Your data stays yours.

  • CAD files stay with you

    Only extracted data and preview images leave your network. The native files never do.

  • Read-only, always

    Alloy Catalog never changes your CAD files, vault or ERP. If it is ever unavailable, your work carries on.

  • Free to leave

    Export everything as CSV or JSON at any time. Your data is never used to train AI models, and you choose the AI provider.
